# Billing and refunds

Synthetic policy text for the support-triage laboratory. It is not real advice.

## Duplicate charges

A duplicate charge for the same invoice is refunded in full without further
approval from the customer. Confirm the invoice number, state that the
duplicate charge is refunded, and say that banks usually show it within five
business days.

## Partial-month refunds

A cancellation part-way through a paid period does not create a refund. Access
continues until the period ends. Say this plainly instead of promising a credit.

## Final amounts

When a customer asks for a final amount, quote the amount already invoiced for
the current period and state that no further charge follows the cancellation.
