# Product support

Synthetic policy text for the support-triage laboratory. It is not real advice.

## Empty export files

An export that finishes with only a header row is a known defect in the report
range filter. Ask for the exact report name and date range, then say the defect
is recorded for engineering. Do not promise a delivery date.

## Inviting teammates

Workspace owners invite colleagues from Settings, then Members, then Invite.
Viewer seats are included in every plan and do not change the amount charged.

## Escalation

Escalate to a human reviewer when a ticket reports lost data, a security
concern, or a charge the policy text does not cover.
