# Regional Bird Atlas: Blackwater Marsh Entry

Conflicts-With: marsh-survey-2024

The atlas records 96 grey heron nesting pairs at Blackwater Marsh. The entry
is drawn from the 2019 county-wide colonial waterbird return and has not been
revised since, because the atlas publishes on a ten-year cycle.

The atlas counts a heronry from the roadside, where it can be seen without
entering the reserve. That rule excludes the willow scrub east of the
boardwalk. The entry does not state which heronries were visited.
