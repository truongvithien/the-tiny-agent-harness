# Reserve Warden Field Notes, April 2024

The count sheet for 11 April was lost when the boardwalk hide flooded. The
morning's tallies for the northern carr were never transcribed, so this
notebook holds no total for that date and no total for the season.

Notes from 12 April onward record weather, tide, and observer names only. Any
figure for 2024 has to come from the survey report, not from these notes.
